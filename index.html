i · 場地與裝備i · Court & gear

先認識這片場地Know the court

匹克球融合了網球、羽球與桌球的元素。球場與羽球雙打場地一樣大：長 13.4 公尺、寬 6.1 公尺（44 × 20 英尺），單打與雙打共用同一個場地。

Pickleball blends elements of tennis, badminton and table tennis. The court is the same size as a badminton doubles court, 13.4 m by 6.1 m (44 by 20 ft), and singles and doubles share it.

13.4 × 6.1m場地長 × 寬，同羽球雙打場Court length × width, same as badminton doubles
2.13m廚房區（非截擊區）：自網前起 7 英尺，橫跨全場寬Kitchen (non-volley zone): 7 ft from the net, full court width
91.4 / 86.4cm球網高度：兩側 / 中央（36 吋 / 34 吋）Net height: sidelines / center (36 in / 34 in)
4.57m發球區深度（15 英尺）Service-court depth (15 ft)

裝備：球拍與球Gear: paddle and ball

匹克球使用實心球拍（木質或複合材質）與有孔洞的塑膠球，不是帶網線的球拍。

Pickleball uses a solid paddle (wood or composite) and a perforated plastic ball, not a strung racquet.

ii · 三條黃金規則ii · The three golden rules

記住這三條，就能開打Remember these three and you can play

新手最常出包的，就是這三件事：怎麼發球、前兩球怎麼接、廚房區怎麼站。

Beginners slip up on three things: how to serve, how to handle the first two shots, and where to stand around the kitchen.

i

下手發球Underhand serve

球拍由下往上揮擊，擊球瞬間球不得高於腰部。細節（兩種發球與腳位）請看下一頁。

Swing the paddle upward from below, and the ball must be no higher than your waist at contact. Both serve types and footwork are on the next page.

ii

雙跳原則Two-bounce rule

發球要讓接發球方彈一次再回擊；接發球的回球，發球方也要讓它彈一次再打。這前兩拍之後，才能凌空截擊。

The serve must bounce once before the receiver hits it, and the return must bounce once before the serving side hits it. Only after those two bounces may anyone volley.

iii

廚房區The kitchen

網前每側 7 英尺（2.13 公尺）是廚房區（非截擊區），區域邊線也算在內。在裡面凌空擊球是違例。

The 7 ft (2.13 m) strip on each side of the net is the kitchen (non-volley zone), and its boundary lines count as part of it. Volleying from inside it is a fault.

最常被誤解的一條The most misunderstood rule

廚房區不是「禁區」，只是不能在裡面凌空The kitchen is not off-limits. You just can’t volley in it

很多人以為球要先彈進廚房，自己才能踏進去。實際上，你隨時都可以進入廚房區；限制只有一個：凌空擊球（球沒落地就打）時，腳、身體或身上的東西不能碰到廚房區，包括踩線，也包括擊球後衝力把你帶進去。

Many people think the ball must bounce in the kitchen before they may step in. In fact you can enter the kitchen at any time. There is one restriction: when you volley (hit the ball before it bounces), you may not touch the kitchen, including its lines, and momentum that carries you in after the hit is also a fault.

碰到廚房區後，要雙腳都完全離開，才可以再次凌空擊球。

After touching the kitchen, both feet must be fully out of it before you may volley again.

iii · 發球iii · The serve

發球：兩種合法方式The serve: two legal ways

每次只有一次發球機會；發球必須由下往上，越過對方廚房區，落入斜對角的發球區。

You get a single attempt. The serve goes underhand, over the opponent’s kitchen, and into the diagonal service court.

凌空發球 Volley serveVolley serve

球還沒落地就擊出Hit the ball out of the air

球拍必須沿著明顯向上的弧線揮擊。

The paddle must travel in a clear upward arc at contact.

擊球瞬間，球拍頭的最高點不得高於手腕。

At contact, the highest point of the paddle head must not be above your wrist.

擊球瞬間，球不得高於腰部。

At contact, the ball must be no higher than your waist.

三者缺一，就是發球違例。

Missing any one of the three is a serve fault.

落地發球 Drop serveDrop serve

讓球彈起後再擊Let the ball bounce, then hit it

用單手自然高度放開球，不可向任何方向施力推出去。

Release the ball from natural height with one hand, and do not push it in any direction.

讓球彈起後再擊球，不受上面三項拍位、弧線與腰部高度限制。

Hit it after the bounce. The upward-arc, wrist and waist limits above do not apply.

兩種發球放球時，都不能對球加旋或做手法。

For both serves, the release must not add spin or manipulate the ball.

iv · 計分與報分iv · Scoring & calling

只有發球方能得分Only the server scores

匹克球採發球方得分制（Side-Out Scoring）。接發球方贏了這一球，不加分，只是拿到發球權。

Pickleball uses side-out scoring. If the receiving side wins the rally, it gets no point, only the serve.

怎樣算贏How to win

一般比賽打到 11 分，且必須領先對手至少 2 分，例如 11-9、12-10。

Games are normally played to 11, and you must lead by at least 2, for example 11–9 or 12–10.

單打Singles

發球方的分數是偶數（含 0）就從右側發球，奇數就從左側發球；每得一分，換到另一側繼續發。報分只報兩個數字：發球方分數、接發方分數。

The server serves from the right when their score is even (including 0) and from the left when odd, switching sides after every point they win. Call two numbers: server’s score, then receiver’s score.

雙打報分：三個數字Doubles: three numbers

每次發球前，必須把整個比分報完：發球方分數、接發方分數、發球員序號（1 或 2）。

Before every serve, call the whole score: serving team’s score, receiving team’s score, then the server number (1 or 2).

報分Call意思Meaning
0-0-2開局。只有一位發球員，所以是「2」。Game start. Only one server, so it is “2”.
2-1-1我方 2 分、對方 1 分，由我方第 1 發球員發球。We have 2, they have 1, and our first server is serving.
4-3-2我方 4 分、對方 3 分，由我方第 2 發球員發球。We have 4, they have 3, and our second server is serving.

球沒打好沒關係，報分報錯才會被罰：整個比分必須在發球前報完。A bad shot is just a bad shot, but the whole score has to be called before the serve is hit.

依據：USA Pickleball 2026 官方規則書 4.B、5.A、6.B、6.C；USA Pickleball「Scoring & position」。開局報分以規則書的「0-0-2」為準。Sources: USA Pickleball 2026 Official Rulebook 4.B, 5.A, 6.B, 6.C; USA Pickleball “Scoring & position”. The opening call follows the rulebook’s “zero-zero-two”.

v · 雙打輪轉v · Doubles rotation

誰發球、站哪邊？按按看Who serves, and from where? Try it

雙打的輪轉看似複雜，其實只有一個動作會「換位置」：發球方得分。按下面的按鈕，看站位與報分怎麼變。

Doubles rotation looks complicated, but only one thing moves players: the serving team scoring. Press the buttons and watch positions and the score call change.

A 隊面向右，右手邊在下方；B 隊面向左，右手邊在上方。發球一律斜對角（右對右、左對左）。Team A faces right, so its right-hand court is at the bottom; Team B faces left, so its right-hand court is at the top. Serves always go diagonally (right to right, left to left).

發球員Server接發球員Receiver
1

得分：換邊Point won: swap

發球方得分，兩位隊友左右互換，同一人繼續發球。接發球方不動。

When the serving team scores, the two partners switch sides and the same player serves again. The receivers don’t move.

2

失誤：原地接手Fault: stay put

第 1 發球員失誤，搭檔站在原位接手發球（第 2 發球員）。輸球不會讓任何人換位。

When the first server faults, the partner serves from where they stand (second server). Losing a rally never moves anyone.

3

換發：右側先發Side-out: right first

第 2 發球員也失誤，發球權換到對方，由對方站在右側的球員先發。例外：每局開頭只有一位發球員。

When the second server faults too, the serve passes to the other team, and the player on its right serves first. The exception: only one server at the start of each game.

常見誤解A common mix-up

「第 2 發球員，偶數分站右、奇數分站左」並不是規則“Second server: even score on the right, odd on the left” is not the rule

規則書的站位依據是：本局最先發球的那位球員，在隊伍得分為偶數（含 0）時站右側，奇數時站左側；他的搭檔站另一側。第 2 發球員就是站在原位的搭檔，所以像「0-2-2」這種偶數分，第 2 發球員是站在左側發球的。

The rulebook ties positions to one player: the team’s starting server stands on the right when the team score is even (including 0) and on the left when odd, with the partner on the other side. The second server is simply the partner where they stand, so at an even score such as 0-2-2 the second server serves from the left.

vi · 基本功與違例vi · Skills & faults

四個基本功，加上四種違例Four basic skills and four faults

左邊是教練課的教學重點（技術觀點，不是規則書內容）；右邊是規則書列出的常見違例。

On the left are the coach’s teaching notes (technique, not rulebook content). On the right are common faults listed in the rulebook.

教練教學重點Coach’s teaching notes

一、大陸式握拍（Continental）1. Continental grip

像跟球拍握手一樣自然握住；正手、反手不用轉換握法，反應最快，適合吊球與截擊。

Hold the paddle as if shaking hands with it. You can hit forehand and backhand without changing grip, which suits dinks and volleys.

二、廚房輕吊球（Dink）2. Dink

在廚房線前輕輕推球，讓球落進對方的廚房區，逼對手先失誤。

A soft shot at the kitchen line that lands in the opponent’s kitchen and waits for them to make the first mistake.

三、底線抽球（Groundstroke）3. Groundstroke

球落地後的常規回擊，重點是重心轉移與穩定的擊球點。

A normal shot after the bounce. Focus on weight transfer and a steady contact point.

四、凌空截擊（Volley）4. Volley

在廚房線後方、球落地前直接攔截，動作短而乾脆。

Hit the ball out of the air from behind the kitchen line with a short, compact motion.

規則書列出的常見違例Common faults from the rulebook

廚房區違例Kitchen fault

凌空擊球時碰到廚房區（含線、夥伴或身上物品），或衝力把你帶進去。

Touching the kitchen (lines, your partner or anything you carry included) while volleying, or being carried in by momentum.

違反雙跳Two-bounce fault

前兩拍沒等球落地就凌空擊球。

Volleying before the first two bounces have happened.

發球違例Serve fault

腳位不對、擊球位置或拍位不合規、落點在廚房區或廚房線、或沒報完分。

Wrong footwork, illegal contact or paddle position, a landing in the kitchen or on its line, or not calling the score.

出界與觸網Out and net faults

球落在對方場地之外，或身體、球拍在球仍在比賽中時碰到球網。

The ball landing outside the opponent’s court, or touching the net with your body or paddle while the ball is live.
